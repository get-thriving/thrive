# Collaborate on an Entity

This how-to walks through sharing a workspace entity with another Thrive user,
responding to invites and access requests, and ending access. Recipients need
their own Thrive account.

For background, see [Collaboration](../concepts/collaboration.md). For public
guest links (no account), see [Share an Entity](share-an-entity.md) instead.

## Invite Someone (As Owner)

1. Sign in and open the entity you want to share (for example a
   [vacation](../concepts/vacations.md) or [habit](../concepts/habits.md)).
2. Open the **Access** section on the leaf panel.
3. Click **Invite**, search for the user by name or email, and select them.
4. Choose an access level: **Reader**, **Commenter**, or **Writer**.
5. Confirm the invite.

The other person gets access right away and sees a pending invite under
**Core → Collaboration → Invites**.

To change or remove their access later, return to the Access section on the
entity and update or remove the grant.

## Acknowledge An Invite (As Recipient)

1. Open **Core → Collaboration** (or the Collaboration widget on the home
   page).
2. On the **Invites** tab, open the invite or use the quick actions on the
   card.
3. **Acknowledge** to clear the pending invite, or **Cancel** if you are the
   owner withdrawing it before they acknowledge.

You can open the shared entity from Collaboration or from the usual feature
list (for example Vacations), where an owner chip marks items that belong to
someone else.

## Ask For Access

1. Open a link to an entity you cannot access.
2. On the **Access Denied** screen, choose **Ask for read access** or **Ask
   for write access**.
3. Wait for the owner to accept or reject under **Core → Collaboration → Asked
   of you**.
4. Track your request under **Asking**.

## Accept Or Reject A Request (As Owner)

1. Open **Core → Collaboration → Asked of you**.
2. Review who is asking, for which entity, and at which level.
3. **Accept** to grant access, or **Reject** to decline.

## Stop Collaborating

### As owner

1. Open the entity’s **Access** section.
2. Remove the collaborator’s grant (or lower their level if you only want to
   restrict them).

### As collaborator

1. Open the shared entity’s **Access** section, or open the grant under
   **Core → Collaboration → Shared with you**.
2. Click **Forget** and confirm.

Forget removes your access to that entity. It does not delete the owner’s
copy.

## Tips

* Prefer inviting at the **parent** when you mean to share a whole tree (doc
  folder, smart list, metric). Children often inherit access.
* Use **Reader** for visibility-only sharing and **Writer** when the other
  person should edit.
* Keep an eye on **Core → Collaboration** (or the home widget) so invites and
  requests do not sit unanswered.
* Collaboration is between Thrive users. For people without accounts, use
  [Publish](../concepts/publish.md).
