# ADR 0020: Strata unit layout

## Status

Accepted (2026-11-09)

## Context

With Strata on ([ADR 0019](0019.ui-styles.md)), the panels of a screen sit side
by side instead of over each other. A screen opens two to four of them (list,
item, and what opens from the item), and later more, as sections of the item
become panels of their own. They share a window whose width runs from about
900px to over 2500px.

Two earlier attempts did not hold up. Giving every panel a pixel width and
letting one take the leftover room needed a rule per screen for which panel is
the big one, and left the window empty or cramped depending on its width.
Letting the user drag boundaries moves the problem to the user.

## Decision

**The window is split into units, and every panel is a number of them.**

- The width minus the rail is `X` equal units: `floor(width / 175)`, at most
  12. That is 7 at 1280px and 1440px, 10 at 1920px and 12 at 2560px, where
  units get wider than 175px. The 176px labelled rail costs up to a unit.
- A panel declares a size on its route `handle`: **S** is 2 units, **M** is 3,
  **L** is 5, so at 1440px a small panel is about 400px, a medium one 600px and
  a large one 1000px. **FULL** is all `X` units, for pages that want the whole
  window, such as the home page and working memory. It may also be marked
  `grow`. Routes without a declaration get trunk M, branch M, tool M, leaf S,
  leaflet S, and none grow.
- The panels the URL opens are the **path**, left to right: trunk, branch, leaf,
  leaflet, from the matched routes' `displayType`. A tool page stands where a
  trunk would.
- One panel is **focused**, by default the deepest. It always shows whole, and
  is squeezed to `X` if it is bigger than the screen.
- The other panels are added outward from the focused one, the child first then
  the parent, as long as each fits whole. The first that does not gets the
  leftover units and is a **peek**: its content is laid out at its full size
  and pinned to the right edge, so the panel looks shifted left with only its
  right part showing, dimmed and striped over. When it comes back whole it
  slides in from the left rather than growing. Everything further out folds to a 10px
  **spine**. Clicking a spine or a peek focuses it, which brings it in whole.
- If the whole path fits, spare units go to panels marked `grow`, rightmost
  first, one size step at most: S to M is 1 unit, M to L is 2 and L to XL, which
  is 7 units, is 2. What is still left is split in two, as empty slots to the
  left and the right of the row, so the row sits in the middle of the window.
- `h` and `l` move the focus one panel left or right, across whole panels,
  peeks and spines, and the layout is recomputed so the newly focused panel is
  whole. The focused panel, when there is more than one open, has an amber top
  edge and takes the keyboard focus, so Tab goes on from there. The keys do
  nothing while the user types in an input, a text area, a select or the note
  editor, inside a dialog or a menu, or with a modifier held.
- `Escape` closes the focused panel, or the only one open, by using the close
  control the panel already has, so its exit animation, the filters it keeps
  and the unsaved-changes check are the panel's own. A panel with no close
  control, such as the home page, ignores it. It is left alone where `h` and
  `l` are: in a field, a dialog or a menu, which have `Escape` for themselves,
  and with a modifier held. Closing a panel closes what is open from it too, as
  its close control does.
- Focus resets to the deepest panel when the path changes, so opening an item
  shows it. Moving to another item on the same screen keeps it.

The engine is pure (`infra/strata/units.ts`) and has no React in it. A thin
provider in the workspace route measures the window, runs it, and gives each
panel its slot. Width changes animate for 180ms, and not at all under
`prefers-reduced-motion`. `?strata-debug=1` draws the unit grid over the
columns, with the layout as text, to compare against.

Panels render inside their parents in the route tree, so `inert` cannot be put
on a folded or peeked panel without also disabling the panels nested in it. A
folded panel is `visibility: hidden` instead, which removes it from the tab
order and the accessibility tree, and each nested column sets itself visible
again. Tabbing into a peek focuses that panel, which brings it in whole.

### Answers to the open questions

1. When a satellite does not fit, **skip** it and try the next one.
2. Satellites take spare units **before** growth does.
3. Item panels (leaves) are **S** for now, including metric entry, smart list
   item, habit, chore and time plan activity; they will shrink as forms before
   they grow.
4. The inbox task gets **no** Note satellite in this work.
5. A pinned satellite **stays pinned** when moving to another item on the same
   screen.
6. A unit is **175px** wide, with a maximum of **12**, and the sizes are S 2,
   M 3, L 5 units. They are the starting numbers, kept in one place so they
   can be tuned.

### Not in this ADR yet

Satellites (sections of an item as panels), `j` and `k` moving along the rows
of a list, a footer with the keys that work right now, and personal overrides
of the numbers. They extend this model and will be added
here as they land.

## Consequences

- One rule for every screen. What differs between screens is a size and a grow
  flag per route, in the route's own file.
- Until satellites exist, a screen with a big window shows a lot of empty room,
  split to both sides of the row.
- A window that cannot fit the focused panel and its neighbours folds the
  outer ones to spines, and a peek shows part of a panel the user cannot use
  until it is focused.
- The leaf expansion buttons have no place in the layout and are hidden with
  Strata on. Classic keeps them.
